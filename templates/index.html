The best part of a trip is the detour.

Drop in a city or a photo. Gemma 4 plans four small adventures that fit your mood and your free hours, plus a way to meet someone new along the way.

Saved quests

Tap Save on any quest and it will wait for you here.

How it works

  1. You show or tellUpload a photo or type where you are.
  2. Flask asks Gemma 4Our Python backend sends your photo, mood and time to the model.
  3. You get questsFour ranked detours, each with a way to meet people.

SideQuest is open source (MIT). Built at Hacktoberfest Hack Day Chennai with Gemma 4.
